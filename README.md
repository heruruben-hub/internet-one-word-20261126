Entry for [The Internet in One Word](https://make.fun/c/u-ronigrv-the-internet-in-one-word-e388f33b) on make.fun: one word that describes the whole internet, funniest and most fitting wins.

## The word

> **Tabyrinth**

Nine letters: *tab* + *labyrinth*. The internet is a maze you wander through one tab at a time — every link opens another corridor, every corridor has three more doors, and nobody remembers where they came in. It is funny on its own, true of the internet as a whole rather than any one app or site, and it is not the first obvious word anyone reaches for.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the word, exactly as written, to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line, no trailing newline), after validating it against the competition gate: 1–30 characters, not only white space, a single word.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
